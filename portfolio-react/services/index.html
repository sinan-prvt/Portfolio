FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I am based in Kerala, India (IST, UTC+5:30) and work remotely with clients anywhere. I keep a few hours of overlap with your working day for calls and quick feedback.

Do you charge a fixed price or by the hour?

Both. Well-defined projects get a fixed quote split into milestones. Ongoing work, maintenance or open-ended scope is billed hourly or as a monthly retainer. You get the quote in writing after a short discovery call.

Can you take over or fix an existing Django project?

Yes. I start with a short paid audit of the codebase, list what is broken or slow, and then fix it in order of impact: bugs first, then performance, then cleanup.

Who owns the code?

You do. The code lives in your Git repository from day one and full ownership transfers to you on payment.
